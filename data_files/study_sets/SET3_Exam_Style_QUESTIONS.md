**29. Symmetric cipher structure**

**30. Finite field arithmetic and AES**

a) Define a group, a ring and a field, stating precisely what distinguishes a field from a ring. **(5 marks)**

c) Explain why the modulus polynomial in GF(2^n) must be irreducible, drawing an explicit parallel with the requirement that p be prime in GF(p). **(4 marks)**
