## Permission and source publication

The app uses a custom [source-available licence](LICENSE). Independent forks and derivative work require the owner's prior written permission. For authorized derivatives, publish the complete corresponding source under the same terms, retain attribution and notices, identify your changes, and notify the owner as required by the licence. Merely notifying the owner is not permission.

GitHub's terms separately permit platform copies of public repositories. A GitHub fork does not by itself authorize an independent derivative project under this licence.
